Key insights & recommendations

  1. Contract type is the biggest driver. Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7%, versus 11.3% on one-year and 2.8% on two-year contracts.
  2. Churn happens early. Customers in their first 6 months churn at 53%, falling to under 10% after 4 years.
  3. Fiber optic and higher bills. Fiber customers churn at 41.9% (DSL 19%), and churned customers pay a higher median bill ($79.6 vs $64.4).
  4. Support and payment method matter. Customers without Tech Support churn at 41.6% versus 15.2% with it, and electronic check users churn at 45.3%.
    • Incentivise month-to-month customers to move to 1-2 year contracts
    • Run onboarding and check-ins during the first 3-6 months
    • Review fiber pricing and service quality
    • Bundle Tech Support and Online Security for new customers
    • Encourage automatic payment methods
